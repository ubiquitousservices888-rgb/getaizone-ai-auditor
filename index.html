TRANSPARENCY

What this MVP is and is not.

GetAIZone currently performs deterministic, browser-based rule evaluation. It does not inspect private accounts, scan company networks, verify licenses, access vendor billing systems, or guarantee cost reductions. It is an assessment and prioritization tool, not a substitute for professional legal, cybersecurity, financial, or compliance advice.
